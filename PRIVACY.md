# Privacy

These plugins collect nothing. They have no analytics, no telemetry and no server of their own.

## What stays on your machine

The plugins keep their files under `~/.claude/plugins/config/claude-for-tax/`: your practice profile, matter folders, verification logs, and the workbooks and dashboards they export.

## What leaves your machine

- **Your conversation with Claude.** The plugins run inside Claude Code or Claude Cowork, so the documents and figures you work on are sent to Anthropic under your Anthropic account's terms and [privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
- **Connectors you choose to connect.** Each plugin lists Google Drive and Slack as optional connectors (`tax-builder-hub` lists only Slack). They are run by Google and Slack, not by these plugins, and nothing is sent to them until you connect them and a skill reads or posts through them. Their own privacy policies apply: [Google](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Slack](https://slack.com/trust/privacy/privacy-policy).
